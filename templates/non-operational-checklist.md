# 非操作性合规与发布前检查清单 / Non-Operational Compliance & Pre-Release Checklist

此检查清单为概念性、非操作性的项目准备清单，旨在帮助团队在将内容或服务面向中国用户前完成合规性与本地化的基本准备工作。请将每一项作为需要进一步评估或咨询的指引，而非可执行步骤。

Checklist（中文）
- 法律与合规咨询：已咨询具有相关资质的法律/合规顾问（是/否）
- 数据分类：已识别个人数据与敏感数据类别并拟定最小化策略（是/否）
- 跨境数据评估：如涉及跨境传输，是否已计划进行合规性评估并咨询专家（是/否）
- 本地化文案：隐私政策、服务条款、用户协议等已准备本地化草案并提交本地法律审阅（是/否）
- 支付与结算：已评估本地支付需求并初步筛选合规的支付服务提供商（是/否/不适用）
- 客服与运营：已制定本地化客服流程、投诉/申诉流程与时区支持计划（是/否）
- 内容治理：已搭建或设计内容治理与 UGC 管理的高层流程与应急预案（是/否）
- 第三方合同：已审查云服务、CDN、第三方厂商的服务协议中的合规与数据处理条款（是/否）
- 风险记录：已记录识别的主要合规风险与拟采取的缓解措施（是/否）
- 仓库合规性：仓库内容中不包含任何可用于规避监管、翻墙或绕过访问控制的操作性指南或工具（确认）

Checklist (English)
- Legal & compliance counsel: Consulted qualified legal/compliance counsel (Yes/No)
- Data classification: Identified categories of personal and sensitive data and drafted minimization strategies (Yes/No)
- Cross-border data assessment: If cross-border transfers are involved, planned a compliance assessment and consulted experts (Yes/No)
- Localized legal texts: Drafted localized privacy policy, terms of service, and user agreements and submitted for local legal review (Yes/No)
- Payments & settlement: Evaluated local payment requirements and shortlisted compliant payment providers (Yes/No/N/A)
- Support & operations: Defined localized support workflows, complaint/escalation processes, and timezone coverage (Yes/No)
- Content governance: Designed high-level content governance and UGC moderation workflows and contingency plans (Yes/No)
- Third-party contracts: Reviewed vendor contracts (cloud, CDN, third parties) for compliance and data processing clauses (Yes/No)
- Risk register: Documented major compliance risks and proposed mitigation measures (Yes/No)
- Repository check: Confirm repository contains no operational circumvention guides, scripts, or tools (Confirm)

提示 / Notes
- 此清单为概念性参考；每一项均可能需要进一步的法律、合规或安全专家参与以形成可执行的合规计划。
- Do not store or share this checklist as an approval to bypass local laws or operational requirements. Always proceed with legal counsel where applicable.
