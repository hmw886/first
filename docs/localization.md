# 本地化与国际化策略 / Localization & Internationalization (High-level)

## 中文说明
本文件提供面向中国用户进行内容与产品本地化的高层次建议，涵盖翻译、文化适配、支付与客服等方面。该文件为概念性指南，不包含任何具体配置、脚本或可用于规避监管的操作步骤。

要点：
- 语言质量与审校：采用专业翻译与本地化审校流程，确保术语一致性与文化适配。建议建立术语表与风格指南（Style Guide）。
- 法律文案本地化：隐私政策、服务条款与用户协议等法律文本应由具备本地法律资质的专业人士审阅并对照当地法规进行本地化修改。
- 支付与结算：根据目标用户常用支付方式（如支付宝、微信支付等）评估接入需求并审查支付服务提供商的合规条款（概念性讨论）。
- 客服与运营：建立本地化客服流程与时区支持，考虑本地节假日、服务时段与用户期望。提供中文支持渠道与清晰的投诉/申诉流程。
- 性能与体验：本地化不仅是语言翻译，还包括时区、货币、格式（日期/数字）、内容排序与推荐策略的本地化优化。

## English (High-level)
This document provides high-level recommendations for localizing content and products for users in China. It covers translation and cultural adaptation, legal document localization, payments, customer support, and user experience considerations. This is a conceptual guide and contains no operational configurations or instructions that could be used to circumvent regulations.

Key points:
- Translation quality and review: Use professional translators and a localization review process. Maintain a glossary and style guide for consistent terminology.
- Legal texts localization: Localize privacy policies, terms of service, and user agreements with review by local legal professionals to ensure compliance with local regulations.
- Payments and settlement: Assess requirements for integrating common local payment methods and review payment providers’ compliance terms (conceptual discussion only).
- Customer support and operations: Provide localized support channels, consider local timezones and holidays, and offer clear complaint/escalation processes in Chinese.
- Performance & UX: Localization includes timezone, currency, date/number formats, and tailoring content ranking/recommendation for local preferences.

提示（Recommendations）：
- 在本地化流程中将合规性与隐私考虑纳入，确保用户数据处理符合当地法律要求。
