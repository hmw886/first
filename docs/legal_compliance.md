# 合规要点概览 / Legal & Compliance (High-level)

## 中文（概览）
本文件提供面向中国用户发布内容或服务时的一些高层次合规要点与注意事项，旨在帮助团队识别需要进一步咨询法律或合规专家的领域。该文件仅作概念性说明，不包含任何申请流程、表格、脚本或操作性细节。

主要议题：
- 监管与许可概念：了解针对公众互联网信息服务的监管要求（如备案/许可概念），以及与所提供内容或服务类型相关的行业监管（例如金融、教育、医疗等）。
- 数据分类与跨境传输：识别个人信息与敏感数据，制定数据最小化与访问控制策略；了解跨境传输的合规要求并咨询法律意见与合规评估服务（例如数据出境评估）。
- 内容合规与治理：制定内容管理规则、用户生成内容（UGC）管理流程与应对机制，确保有明确的责任分工与法律顾问渠道。
- 商业合规：税务、支付合规与消费者保护要求等（依据所属行业与服务模式而异）。
- 合同与第三方服务商：与云服务商、CDN、支付机构签署合同比较、审查服务协议中的合规条款与数据处理条款。

## English (High-level)
This file outlines high-level legal and compliance considerations when publishing content or services to users in China. It is intended to help teams identify areas where further legal or compliance consultation is necessary. No operational procedures, forms, scripts, or application steps are provided.

Key topics:
- Regulatory and licensing concepts: Understand the regulatory landscape for public internet information services and industry-specific rules (e.g., finance, education, healthcare).
- Data classification and cross-border transfer: Identify personal and sensitive data, implement data minimization and access control principles, and consult legal counsel regarding cross-border data transfer assessments.
- Content compliance and governance: Establish content governance policies, UGC moderation workflows, and escalation channels with clear responsibilities.
- Commercial compliance: Consider tax, payment, and consumer protection obligations relevant to your service and business model.
- Contracts and third-party vendors: Review vendor contracts (cloud, CDN, payment providers) for compliance-related clauses and data processing agreements.

---

提示（Recommendations）
- 在任何跨境部署或数据交换前，请进行法律尽职调查（Legal Due Diligence）并咨询合规专家。
- 将合规性要求纳入产品设计（Privacy by Design / Compliance by Design）流程，尽早识别风险并记录决策。
